About the atlas

EN-A and EN-B retain the review’s venue groups; they do not label a paper’s language. Chinese includes simplified and traditional characters. Japanese and Korean studies also cover kana, Hangul and mixed scripts.

These two reading views can overlap. Shared papers appear in both; the complete atlas contains 241 unique records.

Source annotations and the archive do not establish input performance beyond the original studies.
